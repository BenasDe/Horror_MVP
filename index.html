📜 CULTIVATOR'S COMPENDIUM • GAMEPLAY MANUAL

Survival doctrine and forbidden talisman arts for escaping the 26-floor Blood Marrow Pagoda.

🏃 Grid Movement

Desktop: Use W A S D or Arrow Keys to step cardinally across the 9x9 grid.

Mobile: Tap the Virtual D-Pad or swipe directly anywhere on the canvas to leap.

Your step direction sets your facing arrow, which dictates the trajectory of directional spells.

⚠️ Rooted While Channeling

When you prime a talisman, your rogue soul is rooted completely in place until the spell detonates.

Never start channeling when a Jiangshi is already winding up to hop into your tile!

🩸 Soul Hits & Qi Stamina

You begin with only 1 Soul Hit! A single fatal touch extinguishes your soul.

Floor Qi Stamina: Talismans draw on limited Qi each floor. Slay fiends or reach Sanctuaries to replenish stamina.

💀 Corrupted Marrow

Slaying corpse fiends extracts precious Corrupted Marrow.

Carry your marrow to safe floors to buy permanent stat upgrades and forbidden spells from the vampire shopkeeper.

♟️ Pawn's Stride [Slot 1 / Q]

Strikes 2 tiles straight ahead in your facing direction. Swift 0.5s channel time. Ideal for quick head-on corpse purges.

♞ Knight's Talon [Slot 2 / E]

Bypasses obstacles to strike all 8 L-shaped perimeter tiles (2x1, 1x2). Perfect for clearing enemies closing in around you.

♜ Rook's Severance [Slot 3 / R]

Pierces across the entire floor in all 4 cardinal lines (up, down, left, right). Obliterates entire ranks and files in one blast.

♝ Bishop's Stride

Sends cutting Yin shockwaves along all 4 diagonal lines. Excellent for sniping drifting Wraiths.

♛ Queen's Ruin [Slot 4 / Space]

Unleashes cataclysmic 8-directional cleave across the whole arena. Requires an extreme 3.0s root channel.

Demonic Backlash: If ANY living fiend survives the blast, you sacrifice 1 Soul Hit!

🛡️ Tortoise Sanctuary

Erects Taoist Wards on 4 cardinal tiles around you. Absorbs 1 fatal enemy strike before shattering.

🟢 Jade Green Spikes (Player)

Erupted by your talismans. 100% harmless to the player and safe to walk on.

Lethal to corpse fiends: any enemy stepping onto or caught in green spikes takes damage instantly!

🔴 Crimson Blood Spikes (Enemy)

Spawned by Jiangshi, Wraiths, Corpse Scribes, and the Emperor. Deadly to your soul.

Stacking Priority: If enemy and player spikes occupy the same tile, enemy blood spikes ALWAYS show on top with warning skulls to keep you safe!

🦴 Bone Pillars & Cages (1-Hit Break)

Inaccessible bone monoliths created by ruins and Corpse Scribes to trap you.

1-Hit Destruction: Any damaging player spell (Pawn, Knight, Rook, Queen) strikes and shatters bones in 1 hit with bone marrow bursts!

🛡️ Cyan Taoist Wards (1-Hit Shield)

Taoist barrier tiles that block enemy penetrations.

Absorbs 1 hit completely and shatters, protecting whatever stood on the tile.

🏛️ Marrow Font Sanctuaries

Located on safe floors: Floors 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25.

All demonic pressure lifts. All Soul Hits are restored and floor cast Qi is replenished automatically upon entry.

📍 Ascension Checkpoints

Each Sanctuary reached saves a permanent Ascension Checkpoint.

If your soul is extinguished on higher floors, you can revive right at your last checkpoint with all upgrades intact!

🧛 Lady Xiao Yin (Marrow Alchemist)

The mysterious vampire shopkeeper stationed at every fountain.

Trade your harvested Corrupted Marrow to permanently upgrade Max Soul Hits, improve Agility (movement speed), increase Floor Qi Stamina, and purchase high-tier chess talismans.

💀 Floor 26: The Corpse Emperor

The pinnacle of the pagoda. An 8-Hit demonic titan that moves swiftly across strategic thrones, summons fiends every 6s, and gains 2s damage immunity after each hit.

Shatter his seal to complete the demonic ascension cycle!
